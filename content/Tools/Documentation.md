The official Firaxis documentation is very useful if you need to find out esoteric things about UI components, Asset Editor, or other Art. You can find it by going to the Tools section of your Steam library, and installing "Sid Meier's Civilization VI Development Tools". Open that, and its the Documentation link. An online version is also hosted [here](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/wildweegee101/Civ-6-Documentation/blob/main/Civ6Docs.html). Its a long read, with many very unordered sections, so CTRL-F is your friend.
