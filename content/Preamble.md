What is this guide? Its planned to be a community resource that can be a one-stop shop for all of the ways Civ VI can be modded. As it stands, there is a lot of good information out there, but it is fragmented and scattered across Discord servers, CivFanatics Forums, and general community knowledge. As a result, a beginner will struggle to know what to look for, where to look for it, and whether a tutorial is good, or outdated, or belabouring the point. A modder needs to be very motivated to get through all of this, and it definitely hinders the scene.

The aim of this document is to onboard new modders in such a way that they can get to the fun parts of modding! It will slowly build from simple concepts up to advanced concepts, good workflows, and how to mod effectively and fast.

## Who am I?
I am a modder who is relatively new to the scene, having been around for about 2 years. My main project is a port of the civ IV mod Fall From Heaven, which required many new mechanics, in Lua, in database modifiers and new art textures and models. As a result, I've taken a long journey through the modding eco-system, and cursed all the pitfalls that hampered me along the way, and this guide will hopefully stop you suffering the same problems.

## How can I help?
There are areas that I lack the knowledge on, and can't speak with any decent authority. Audio modding with wWise, the Landmarks system (the arrangement of buildings and districts), DLL modding. If you are reading this guide, and do know how to do these things, and this guide has helped, consider "passing it along" by contributing. This guide is hosted on [Github](https://github.com/slothoth/quartz_civVI_guide) and CivFanatics Forum(On release) and welcomes contributors.

## Civ VII
Civ VII is very similar in a lot of ways to Civ VI, with a few additions and removals. Database modding is almost entirely identical, and so any aspiring Civ VII modders can follow along. I will include a chapter on [[Civ VII relevant differences]] and also reference which chapters are relevant for Civ VII, along with differences and quirks.
